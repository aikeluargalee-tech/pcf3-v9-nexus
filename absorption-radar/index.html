🚨 1. What is the Broken Support Relief Trap?

When Bitcoin breaks below a major structural floor (e.g. $82,500), the initial retest back up into the underside of broken support ($82,200–$82,500) is predominantly retail buy liquidity absorbed by institutional sellers. Never buy into broken support.

🛡️ 2. The Value-Shelf Absorption Gate

A high-conviction swing entry requires price to penetrate into the true institutional value shelf ($75,000–$76,500) AND print a candle with at least a 50% lower wick and close in the upper 35% of the range.

🎯 3. Asymmetric Tranche Execution (30% / 40% / 30%)
