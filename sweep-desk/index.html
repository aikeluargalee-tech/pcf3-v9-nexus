🧠 Smart Money Concepts (SMC) Sweep Desk Methodology

🎯 What is a Liquidity Sweep?

Retail stop-losses and breakout buy/sell stop orders accumulate right beyond visible chart extremes: prior daily highs/lows, weekly swing points, and equal highs/lows (double tops/bottoms). Institutional market makers intentionally push price through these levels to trigger liquidity, absorbing panic sells or breakout buyers without moving the market against themselves.

⚡ Why require a 4-Hour Close Reclaim & Displacement?

A wick through a level is NOT the signal to trade. If price breaks a level and continues with high volume, it is a genuine trend continuation. A valid trade setup only exists if the 4-hour candle closes strictly back inside the range (a reclaim) and prints a large displacement candle body (>1.2x average), proving that institutions absorbed the stops.

🛑 Execution Rule: Buy the Retest, NOT the Wick

Never FOMO buy the wick as it is dropping. Once the 4H candle closes back above the pool with positive spot taker delta, place a limit buy order at the pool level to enter on the retest. Place your invalidation stop loss strictly below the sweep wick low.
