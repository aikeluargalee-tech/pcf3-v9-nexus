🧠 Beginner Jargon Buster & Quantitative Architecture

🔍 What is a "Fractal"?

A repeating geometric pattern across timeframes. Traders observe past formations that share structural characteristics with present conditions, evaluating how market dynamics unfolded. However, a single fractal analog is never a directional guarantee; statistical distributions and macro liquidity must confirm the setup.

🏦 Why did the 2023 Fractal really pump?

Price action was not chart magic. In March 2023, Silicon Valley Bank collapsed, triggering systemic bank run panic. The Federal Reserve immediately stepped in with the Bank Term Funding Program (BTFP), expanding liquidity by over $300B. That emergency liquidity, combined with spot market absorption, drove BTC +47% in two weeks.

⚠️ What happens if liquidity doesn't expand?

If Bitcoin flushes toward $74,000–$78,000 while ETF outflows accelerate and the US Dollar Index (DXY) surges, the fractal thesis invalidates. The price can break down toward $68,000 support. Never trade geometric shapes without confirming spot cumulative volume delta (CVD) and funding rates!
