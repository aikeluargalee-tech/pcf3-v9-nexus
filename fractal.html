🧠 Beginner Jargon Buster: Frequently Asked Questions

🔍 What is a "Fractal"?

A fractal simply means a repeating geometric pattern on a chart. Traders look for past chart shapes that look like today's market, hoping the next move will be the same.

🏦 Why did the 2023 Fractal really pump?

It didn't pump because of the chart drawing! In March 2023, Silicon Valley Bank collapsed, and the US Federal Reserve injected over $300 Billion of emergency liquidity (BTFP). That liquidity drove Bitcoin up +47%.

⚠️ What happens if liquidity doesn't expand?

If Bitcoin drops to $74,000 while ETF money is leaving and the dollar is strengthening, the shape will fail and Bitcoin could slide further down to $68,000. Never trade shapes alone!
