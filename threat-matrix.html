📖 Macro Threat Matrix Jargon & Methodology Guide

What is the SPX / RSP Breadth Divergence (The "Bubble Signal")?

The S&P 500 (SPX) is market-cap weighted, meaning giants like Apple, Microsoft, and Nvidia have outsized influence. The Invesco S&P 500 Equal Weight ETF (RSP) gives every company equal weight. When SPX breaks out while RSP declines, the market breadth is severely broken — the entire stock market is being propped up by fewer than 10 companies while the average stock is already in a bear market.

Why did this setup mirror late 2008?

In late 2008, a virtually identical double-bottom breakout occurred in the SPX/RSP ratio as yields spiked and money panicked into perceived mega-cap safety right before the ultimate 50% liquidation crash.

Why are US 10-Year Treasury Yields > 5.0% toxic for Bitcoin?

When the risk-free US Treasury yield stays above 5.0%, institutional cost of capital remains painfully high. Money market funds offer guaranteed returns, starving high-beta speculative assets like Bitcoin of new institutional capital inflows.

Why does MicroStrategy (MSTR) act as a sentiment canary?

MSTR holds over 250,000 BTC on its corporate balance sheet and trades with embedded leverage. When MSTR fails to reclaim previous cycle highs despite Bitcoin holding temporary support, it signals institutional momentum exhaustion and distribution.
